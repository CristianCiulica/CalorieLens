    02

    How it works

    From plate to numbers.

    Four simple steps, from photo to a full breakdown.

    1. 01

      Take a photo

      Find decent light and frame the whole plate. Home-cooked, restaurant or packaged — it all works.

    2. 02

      Vision analysis

      The plate is split into individual items, then each portion is estimated from visual cues — plate diameter, depth, shadow.

    3. 03

      The breakdown

      In a couple of seconds you get total calories and a protein / carbs / fat split, item by item, with the portion each number assumes.

    4. 04

      Log and track

      Confirm and it's saved. The diary fills itself, and the patterns across days and weeks become obvious without any effort.

    05

    Technology

    Built like an instrument.

    • 01

      One pass, every item

      The plate is segmented before anything is counted, so a mixed bowl is read as its parts instead of one averaged guess.

    • 02

      Portions read from the frame

      Plate diameter, depth and shadow give the model scale, so "a bowl of rice" turns into grams instead of a shrug.

    • 03

      Weak photos are called out

      Blur, glare and hard shadow are checked first. If the frame isn't good enough you get concrete advice instead of a confident wrong number.

    • 04

      Numbers you can audit

      Every total is the sum of items you can see and correct. Nothing hides behind a single score.
